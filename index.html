How this guide works

Positions come from candidates' own websites, the City of Toronto's council voting record, The Local's candidate guides, Build Canada's candidate survey and the UTGSU questionnaire. Yes, Conditional and No are our reading of each written answer, so read the candidate's own words before quoting them. "None found" means we looked and could not find one.

Nothing here is an endorsement.
